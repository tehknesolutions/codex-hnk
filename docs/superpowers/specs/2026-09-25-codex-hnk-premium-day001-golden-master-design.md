# CODEX-HNK Premium Experience — Day 001 · Kether Golden Master

**Status:** DESIGN SPEC — USER-APPROVED DIRECTION
**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Scope:** Premium Experience vertical slice for Day 001 · Kether
**Repository:** `tehknesolutions/codex-hnk`

## 1. Purpose

Transform the existing CODEX-HNK application foundation into the target premium experience without replacing the existing canonical/runtime authority. Day 001 · Kether is the Golden Master: the first complete vertical slice from which the reusable experience system for subsequent Days will be derived.

The product must not feel like a conventional educational dashboard decorated as an occult book. It must behave as a **living digital Codex**: a readable premium grimoire whose material surface reveals interactive, progression-aware digital/metaphysical behavior when meaning or state requires it.

## 2. Approved Product Direction — CODEX VIVO

The approved experience model is:

`Read → Contemplate → Interact → Execute → Record → Progress → Unlock`

The Codex remains the primary object. Quest, XP, Vault, audio, progression and navigation are expressed through the visual and semantic language of that object instead of appearing as unrelated SaaS widgets.

### Core rule

> Technology is hidden inside the grimoire, not placed on top of it.

No generic dashboard/cards merely recolored black and gold. UI chrome must arise from the artifact's own visual grammar.

## 3. Golden Master

**Golden Master:** `Day 001 · Kether`

Day 001 establishes the reusable grammar for the entire Premium Experience:

- editorial hierarchy;
- ritual/progression states;
- navigation;
- material system;
- metaphysical activation;
- motion semantics;
- responsive behavior;
- quest/progression presentation;
- Vault/reflection presentation;
- completion/seal behavior.

The first implementation is a vertical slice, not a blind redesign of all Days. Only after Day 001 reaches the approved target should the reusable system be propagated.

## 4. Visual Regime

Approved balance:

- **70% material grimoire**;
- **30% metaphysical/digital artifact**.

### Material layer

Primary reading and resting states use physical cues:

- deep black field;
- warm parchment/ivory reading surfaces where appropriate;
- restrained metallic gold;
- ink/engraving language;
- illuminated-manuscript framing;
- diagrams and sacred/geometric structures treated as authored editorial material;
- depth and texture without sacrificing text clarity.

### Metaphysical layer

Digital effects appear when the system communicates meaning or state:

- revelation;
- activation;
- progression;
- ritual focus;
- quest transition;
- validation;
- unlocking;
- sealing/completion.

Light, particles, dimensional geometry and emissive behavior are therefore state signals, not constant decoration.

### Three visual modes

1. **Rest — Matter:** reading, orientation, stable navigation.
2. **Interaction — Matter + Light:** hover/focus/selection/ritual engagement.
3. **Manifestation — Metaphysical/Digital:** revelation, quest threshold, validation, seal and unlock.

## 5. Experience Architecture

Macro navigation:

`Tree of Life → Sphere → Day → Threshold → Revelation → Doctrine → Kavanah → Ordeal → Mirror/Vault → Seal → Progression`

The Day is one continuous transforming experience, not seven unrelated pages.

### State 1 — LIMIAR / Threshold

Purpose: transition from navigation into the Day.

Requirements:

- identify Day 001, Kether and its theme;
- establish atmosphere before dense content;
- present one dominant entry action;
- avoid conventional dashboard density;
- support reduced-motion users without losing hierarchy or meaning.

### State 2 — REVELAÇÃO / Revelation

Purpose: open the Codex and introduce the Day as an authored object.

Requirements:

- premium grimoire presentation;
- Day identity and essential premise;
- first meaningful illustration/diagram;
- continuity from Threshold rather than a hard app-page jump.

### State 3 — DOUTRINA / Doctrine

Purpose: sustain deep reading and study.

Requirements:

- long-form typography optimized for comprehension;
- diagrams, references and supporting media integrated into editorial flow;
- no decorative treatment may reduce legibility;
- content structure remains driven by canonical Day content, not invented UI copy;
- desktop and mobile reading must both remain first-class.

### State 4 — KAVANAH

Purpose: move from understanding into focused practice.

Requirements:

- guided practice surface;
- instructions, focus, duration/timing and audio when canonical content provides them;
- visual transition from material reading toward controlled metaphysical activation;
- explicit accessible controls for audio and timing;
- no autoplay dependency for comprehension.

### State 5 — ORDÁLIA / Ordeal

Purpose: turn knowledge into action.

Requirements:

- surface existing quest/runtime authority through the Codex language;
- clearly communicate mission, requirements, progress and result;
- never fabricate completion locally when server/runtime authority disagrees;
- stronger spatial/metaphysical treatment is permitted here because the user is crossing from study into action.

### State 6 — ESPELHO / VAULT

Purpose: record the user's encounter with the Day.

Requirements:

- reflection entry;
- evidence/record surfaces where supported by existing contracts;
- Vault identity integrated into the Codex rather than presented as a detached productivity form;
- autosave/save/error states must be understandable and recoverable;
- user-authored content must remain visually distinct from canonical Codex content.

### State 7 — SELO / Seal

Purpose: close the Day, validate progression and reveal what follows.

Requirements:

- completion is based on existing authority/contracts;
- visual transformation represents validated completion;
- communicate earned progression/XP/unlocks only when supported by runtime state;
- reveal the next legal path without bypassing progression rules;
- completed Day retains a persistent sealed state on later visits.

## 6. Global Shell

The shell must support immersion without trapping the user.

Desktop target:

- persistent but visually quiet Codex navigation;
- Tree of Life/journey access;
- current sphere/day context;
- Vault access;
- progression access where relevant;
- reading area dominates viewport;
- chrome recedes during focused reading/practice.

Mobile target:

- no miniature desktop sidebar;
- reading column remains primary;
- navigation collapses into an intentional Codex control;
- touch targets remain accessible;
- ornamental framing simplifies before content width is sacrificed;
- all seven states remain fully usable, not merely viewable.

## 7. Tree of Life

The Tree is both symbolic structure and product navigation/progression surface.

For the Day 001 slice it must at minimum:

- show Kether as current sphere;
- communicate current Day/progress state;
- distinguish available, completed and locked destinations;
- avoid implying that locked content is executable;
- preserve compatibility with existing progression authority.

The first slice does not require an uncontrolled full 3D rewrite. Dimensional/WebGL treatment may be layered later if it respects performance, accessibility and the same state contract.

## 8. Typography and Editorial Grid

The design system must separate at least:

- monumental/display voice;
- editorial reading voice;
- functional/system voice.

Long-form content determines the grid. Ornament follows the grid; text must never be squeezed to preserve ornament.

Desktop may evoke facing pages/spreads where useful. Mobile must recompose into a single deliberate reading flow rather than scale a spread down.

## 9. Material and Light Semantics

**Black / void:** field, silence, depth, transition.

**Gold / material authority:** hierarchy, authored structure, sacred/editorial emphasis, validated state.

**Parchment / ivory:** sustained reading and human-scale reflection.

**Light / emission:** activation, revelation, progression or manifestation.

Emissive effects cannot become a generic accent applied to every control.

## 10. Motion System

Golden rule:

> No animation exists only to decorate. Every visual change represents meaning, state, progression, revelation or interaction.

Motion categories:

- threshold transition;
- page/material reveal;
- diagram activation;
- Kavanah focus;
- Ordeal threshold/action feedback;
- Vault save/record feedback;
- Seal/completion transformation;
- Tree/progression update.

`prefers-reduced-motion` must preserve all state communication using opacity, hierarchy, static geometry and explicit status copy rather than removing information.

## 11. Existing Authority Must Be Preserved

This redesign is an experience layer over the current CODEX-HNK architecture.

It must not redefine:

- canonical content authority;
- quest completion authority;
- progression legality;
- Vault persistence contracts;
- release/deployment Human Gates;
- source/canon boundaries.

Visual completion is not runtime completion. Local animation is not evidence of progression.

## 12. Content Integrity

The Golden Master must render actual canonical Day 001 content/contracts available in the repository.

The generated visual concept is a **directional concept**, not a source of canonical prose, numerical progression values, sphere ranges, theological statements or quest requirements.

Any text visible in concept imagery that is not backed by repository canon is placeholder visual material and must not silently enter production.

## 13. Accessibility

Minimum product requirements:

- keyboard navigation for all functional controls;
- visible focus state compatible with the visual system;
- semantic headings/landmarks;
- sufficient text/background contrast;
- controls cannot rely solely on color or glow;
- reduced motion support;
- audio has explicit controls and textual equivalent/instructions;
- touch targets appropriate for mobile;
- decorative imagery does not pollute screen-reader flow.

## 14. Performance

Premium must not mean unbounded rendering cost.

Rules:

- prioritize text and interaction readiness;
- progressively enhance expensive visual layers;
- lazy-load non-critical media/3D;
- preserve a complete usable experience when advanced visual effects are unavailable;
- avoid blocking Doctrine reading on cinematic assets;
- mobile receives composition-specific optimization, not only asset downscaling.

## 15. Responsive Behavior

The same semantic state model is shared across platforms, but composition is allowed to differ.

Desktop emphasizes spatial artifact, spread and journey context.

Mobile emphasizes sequential ritual/readability with deliberate transitions between sections.

No required action may exist only through hover.

## 16. Reference Concept

The approved concept direction depicts:

- black/gold Codex shell;
- monumental Kether threshold;
- Tree of Life journey context;
- material open-grimoire Revelation/Doctrine;
- activated Kavanah;
- exploratory/action-oriented Ordeal;
- integrated Mirror/Vault;
- luminous completion Seal.

This reference establishes mood, hierarchy and material/metaphysical balance. It does not prescribe literal generated text, exact geometry, exact assets or inaccessible ornamental density.

## 17. Golden Master Acceptance Criteria

Day 001 is ready to become the template only when all of the following are true:

1. All seven semantic states are represented in one coherent journey.
2. Real canonical Day 001 content is used wherever production content is required.
3. Existing quest/progression/Vault authority remains intact.
4. Desktop and mobile are independently usable and intentionally composed.
5. Deep reading remains comfortable despite premium ornamentation.
6. Reduced-motion and keyboard flows preserve all essential information/actions.
7. Expensive visuals fail gracefully without blocking the Day.
8. Completion/seal cannot be visually or functionally forged by client-only state.
9. The reusable visual/state primitives are separated from Day-001-specific content.
10. Automated tests cover state legality, rendering contracts and critical accessibility behavior.
11. Visual QA compares implementation against the approved concept direction without treating concept-image copy as canon.
12. The result feels like one transforming Codex artifact rather than a collection of themed application screens.

## 18. Propagation Rule

Do not propagate the new experience across all Days until Day 001 passes the Golden Master acceptance criteria.

After acceptance, extract/reuse:

- shell primitives;
- editorial primitives;
- material tokens;
- state-transition primitives;
- Tree/progression presentation;
- Kavanah/Ordeal/Vault/Seal containers;
- accessibility behavior;
- responsive composition rules.

Day-specific canon, quests, media and progression data remain data/content, not hardcoded into the reusable visual system.

## 19. Out of Scope for the First Golden Master Slice

Unless already required by an existing Day 001 contract, this slice does not authorize:

- rewriting all 365 Days;
- replacing existing progression architecture;
- inventing new HNK canon;
- a mandatory full 3D/WebGL Tree rewrite;
- new social/community systems;
- new payment/subscription architecture;
- infrastructure expansion unrelated to the Golden Master experience.
