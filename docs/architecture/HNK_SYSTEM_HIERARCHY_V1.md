# HNK System Hierarchy V1

Status: CREATOR-DIRECTED CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE
Date: 2026-09-27

## Top-level definition

**HNK is the maximum dimension.**

```text
HNK
├── CODEX-HNK — REGISTRAR / GRIMOIRE / TRAIN
├── HNK-KODE  — EXPRESS / MAGIC / PHYSICAL MANIFESTATION LANGUAGE
└── HNK-VERSE — MANIFEST / DIGITAL + ASTRAL + MENTAL
```

## CODEX-HNK

CODEX-HNK is the registry of everything that belongs to HNK and everything that happens in HNK. It is also the HNK grimoire and the HNK training system.

Its knowledge/training scope includes at minimum:

- MAGICO
- ESPIRITUAL
- TECNOLOGICO
- ARTE
- CIENTIFICO

These are knowledge/training vertents of CODEX-HNK, not independent roots above HNK.

## HNK-KODE

HNK-KODE is the language and spiritual idiom of HNK: language for HNK and a manifestation language for intentions from HNK into physical reality — **MAGIC**.

Its domain authority remains distinct from technological implementation details and from runtime manifestation.

## HNK-VERSE

HNK-VERSE is the digital, astral and mental manifestation of HNK.

It consumes governed HNK knowledge/contracts and manifests them as executable systems, worlds, agents, experiences and evidence-producing runtime behavior.

## GID/HNK — Grupo Idealizar

The organizational structure contains three blocks:

```text
GID/HNK / GRUPO IDEALIZAR
├── TEHKNÉ SOLUTIONS
├── SIMPLEWAY ACADEMY
└── HANDS UP CONSULTORIA
```

Organizational ownership and HNK knowledge authority are related but not interchangeable.

## TEHKNÉ-OS placement

TEHKNÉ-OS is the Codex of Tehkné Solutions.

For HNK knowledge architecture its placement is:

```text
HNK
└── CODEX-HNK
    └── TECNOLOGIA
        └── TEHKNÉ SOLUTIONS
            └── TEHKNÉ-OS
```

Therefore TEHKNÉ-OS contributes technological knowledge, methods, evidence and engineering lineage to CODEX-HNK. It is not a second HNK root, and it does not automatically promote Tehkné implementation state into HNK canon.

## Operational flow

```text
TEHKNÉ-OS
  ↓
CODEX-HNK / TECNOLOGIA
  ↓
HNK contracts / governed knowledge
  ↓
HNK-VERSE
  ↓
runtime evidence / learnings
  ↓
CODEX-HNK / TECNOLOGIA review
```

HNK-KODE participates wherever language, spiritual language or MAGIC manifestation contracts are required.

## Core authority rules

1. HNK is the maximum domain.
2. CODEX-HNK registers HNK knowledge/history and provides HNK grimoire/training functions.
3. TEHKNÉ-OS belongs to the CODEX-HNK technological vertent as the Codex of Tehkné Solutions.
4. HNK-KODE owns its language/spiritual-language domain under HNK governance.
5. HNK-VERSE manifests HNK digitally/astrally/mentally and does not self-promote runtime output into canon.
6. Source, evidence, implementation, generated content, approval and canon remain distinct states.
