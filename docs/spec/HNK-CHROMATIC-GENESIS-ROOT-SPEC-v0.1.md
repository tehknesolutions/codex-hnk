# HNK Chromatic Genesis — ROOT SPEC v0.1

Status: CANDIDATE / REQUIRES CANON REVIEW
Authority target: CODEX-HNK ROOT CANON
Consumers: HNK-KODE, haKodan, HNK-VERSE, TEHKNÉ-OS, TKN Design System, Shimokodan/Genius systems

## 0. Intent

Define a provenance-safe symbolic-computational architecture that can connect HNK cosmology, chromatic energies, glyph grammar, Sefer Yetzirah research, Kabbalistic structures, alchemical transformation, astrological correspondences, Genius cognitive strategies and executable haKodan semantics without collapsing historically distinct systems into false equivalence.

The system is inspired in part by the narrative idea of multiple differentiated powers converging into one manifestation, but must remain a proprietary HNK construction. Marvel names, stone identities, glove geometry and recognizable iconography are explicitly non-canonical.

## 1. Authority and promotion

CODEX-HNK remains ROOT CANON for ontological, mathematical, Mandala and fundamental correspondence contracts. HNK-KODE remains DOMAIN CANON for HNK language, lexicon, phonotactics, grammar and computational semantics. haKodan implements HNK-KODE through Semantic IDs, AST, HOM, HNK-IR, adapters, runtimes and manifestation targets.

All proposed correspondences use the governance ladder:

`OBSERVED → DERIVED → HYPOTHESIS → CANDIDATE → VALIDATED → CANONICAL`

External tradition data MUST retain source/provenance. Similarity, shared number, color resemblance or aesthetic affinity never creates canonical identity by itself.

## 2. Genesis pipeline

Candidate macro-pipeline:

`AIN / VOID → ALEF / INTENT → LAW / FORM → YETZIRATIC GRAMMAR → GLYPH → ENERGY → OPERATOR → HNK-KODE → HAKODAN → GENIUS STRATEGY → SHIMOKODAN → GIP → MALKUTH / MANIFESTATION → EVIDENCE`

This is an HNK architecture. Terms borrowed from historical traditions must be tagged by provenance and must not imply that the complete pipeline exists in any external source.

## 3. Fundamental state hypothesis

### 3.1 Chromatic Hexad

Six operational chromatic forces are proposed as a CANDIDATE layer. Their final HNK names, phonology, glyphs, numbers and correspondences MUST be derived only after HNK-KODE and CODEX audits.

Working semantic poles:

1. Amethyst — mind / arcane intelligence / orchestration.
2. Azure — space / architecture / relation / infrastructure.
3. Blood — power / action / execution / force.
4. Emerald — life / adaptation / growth / evolution.
5. Solar/Amber — consciousness / knowledge / illumination / processing.
6. Sixth chromatic force — intentionally UNRESOLVED until correspondence and lexicon research determine a non-arbitrary semantic role.

Canonical visual anchor already used by TKN for Amethyst: `#130583`. This does not by itself canonize the metaphysical correspondence.

### 3.2 Meta-layers

Four non-equivalent meta-principles are proposed:

- **Auric / Gold** — manifestation, convergence, embodiment, value. Candidate semantic bridge to Malkuth/manifestation; NOT declared identical to Malkuth.
- **Octarine** — meta-chromatic transformation, correspondence rewriting and transmutation. This is an HNK redesign inspired by the fictional concept of an additional magical color; it must be marked as external-fiction inspiration plus HNK authorship, never as historical occult doctrine.
- **Titanium** — law, constraint, structure, boundary, precision, form.
- **Obsidian / Void** — potential, silence, unmanifest field, depth.

Together `6 + 4 = 10` creates a decadic structural HYPOTHESIS. Numerical resemblance to the ten sefirot is a research lead only and MUST NOT be promoted as equivalence without validation.

## 4. Sefer Yetzirah research layer

Sefer Yetzirah enters as a major historical research source and generative inspiration, with textual-version provenance required.

Candidate structures to model:

- 10 sefirot / numbers as a historical source structure;
- 22 Hebrew letters;
- 3 Mothers;
- 7 Doubles;
- 12 Simples;
- 32 paths as the traditional composite count of ten sefirot plus twenty-two letters.

### Critical rule

The exact letter-to-element, planet, zodiac, direction, body and other correspondences can vary by recension, manuscript lineage and later interpretive tradition. The HNK Correspondence Engine MUST store tradition/version identifiers and conflicts rather than silently choosing one mapping.

### Candidate computational interpretation

Research whether `3 / 7 / 12` can become three operator families in HNK without claiming that Sefer Yetzirah describes software operators.

- Mothers → foundational/generative operator family candidate.
- Doubles → polarity/state-transition operator family candidate.
- Simples → directional/relational/specialized operator family candidate.

Status: HYPOTHESIS until compared with existing HNK operator ontology.

## 5. Force / Form bridge

Chromatic Genesis MUST preserve the existing HNK Force/Form distinction.

Candidate mapping grammar:

`FORCE` = energetic tendency, transformation pressure, intention vector.

`FORM` = geometry, law, constraint, address, state boundary, manifestation container.

Therefore energy color is never sufficient to define an object. Every canonical Energy Object must include both Force and Form semantics.
