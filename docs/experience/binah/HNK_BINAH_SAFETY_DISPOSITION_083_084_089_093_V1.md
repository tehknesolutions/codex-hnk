## Purpose

## Day 083 — APPROVED_WITH_GUARDS

Source practice: a physical circle of salt or charcoal.

Required production guardrails already represented in the reviewed draft and now frozen as release conditions:

- exit path must remain unobstructed;
- floor must remain stable and non-slip;
- no placement where children or animals can ingest or scatter material;
- no use that damages the floor or creates unsafe residue;
- cleanup is part of completion;
- cord/tape/removable marking may be offered only as an explicitly labeled product fallback when the physical material is unsuitable;
- material movement is never interpreted as evidence of invisible intrusion;
- symbolic protection never substitutes concrete safety.

**Decision:** these constraints are sufficient to clear the material-safety review. No semantic rewrite of the practice is required.

## Day 084 — APPROVED_WITH_GUARDS

Source practice: mirror observation in low light, with candle where the textual execution calls for it.

Required production guardrails already represented in the reviewed draft and now frozen as release conditions:

- blinking, posture adjustment and voluntary stop always remain valid;
- no endurance or intensity scoring;
- stop on ocular pain, dizziness, increasing anxiety, disorientation or difficulty returning to the environment;
- mirror changes remain perceptual/phenomenological data, not proof of entities, diagnosis or objective revelation;
- any real candle must be stable, supervised and separated from hair, paper, fabric and other flammables;
- a soft electric-light mode may exist only as an explicitly labeled product adaptation;
- grounding/return is mandatory before completion.

**Decision:** the reviewed draft contains sufficient controls to clear the production safety gate while preserving the original exercise.

## Day 089 — APPROVED_WITH_GUARDS

Source practice: six hours of verbal silence.

Required production guardrails already represented in the reviewed draft and now frozen as release conditions:

- the window must be scheduled where reasonably compatible with responsibilities;
- necessary speech for safety, health, work, caregiving, consent or emergency communication is always permitted;
- necessary speech does not invalidate the practice;
- silence may not be used to punish, manipulate, ignore an important request or coerce another person into participating;
- no XP depends on perfect silence, isolation or social suffering;
- the practice ends at a defined time and includes deliberate return to speech.

**Decision:** these constraints are sufficient to clear the production safety gate. The six-hour source exercise is preserved without converting legitimate communication into failure.

## Day 093 — HOLD / SAFETY_REVIEW

Source practice: square Pranayama `4-4-4-4`, including inspiratory and expiratory retention.

The reviewed draft already provides strong stop conditions and explicitly refuses to reward breath endurance. However, the retention phases are part of the named canonical pattern. The draft also correctly states that a no-retention variant would be a distinct editorial decision rather than the same canonical execution.

Therefore:

- do not promote the Day merely because stop conditions exist;
- do not silently replace `4-4-4-4` with a no-retention pattern;
- do not require the user to continue through dizziness, dyspnea, pain, increasing anxiety or relevant discomfort;
- interrupted attempts remain valid evidence and never become moral/spiritual failure;
- no XP rewards completed holds, discomfort or persistence.

**Decision:** Day 093 remains `SAFETY_REVIEW` until the canonical production disposition for breath retention is separately approved. This record intentionally does not invent a medical screening protocol or an alternative breathing operator.

## Non-negotiable product rule

A safety fallback may preserve access to the learning objective, but whenever it changes the textual ritual operator it must be labeled as a product adaptation. The HNK runtime must never reward unsafe endurance or present symbolic/subjective effects as objective protection, diagnosis or externally measured causality.
