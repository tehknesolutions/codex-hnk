- Day 090: dispersal, frivolous conversation, justified lies and idea instability are interpreted by function/context rather than turned into rigid language policing; legitimate privacy and evidence-based change of mind remain distinct.
- Day 091: clarity, precise communication, optimism and learning are grounded in evidence and limits; no spiritual/intellectual ranking is created.
- Day 092: false causal links are challenged without denying well-supported causality; the plan's neuroplasticity language is not treated as an individually measured variable without data.
- Day 093: the canonical 4-4-4-4 pattern is preserved, but breath-holding discomfort is a stop condition; thoughts during the exercise are not scored as failure, and no physiological/brain-state claim is inferred without measurement.
