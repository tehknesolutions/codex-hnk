# HNK ROOT / DOMAIN AUTHORITY BOUNDARY V0.1

Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-09-26
Parent research: HNK-RESEARCH GENESIS V0.1

## Rule 1 — Root Canon

CODEX-HNK is the ROOT CANON of HNK.

It owns foundational HNK identities and contracts, including HNK-MATH, Atomic-Kode (AK), Mandala geometry, mathematical transformations, canonical symbolic correspondences and provenance/authority semantics.

## Rule 2 — Linguistic Domain Canon

HNK-KODE is the DOMAIN CANON for operational language concerns: phonology, phonotactics, writing composition, transliteration, corpus, lexicon, grammar and linguistic encoding/decoding.

Domain authority does not permit redefining ROOT-owned identities.

## Rule 3 — Bidirectional Consumption Is Not Bidirectional Authority

CODEX-HNK may consume linguistic projections from HNK-KODE and HNK-KODE may consume foundational contracts from CODEX-HNK.

Consumption does not transfer ownership.

For a field/object, authority follows its declared domain owner, not the repository in which a projection happens to be cached or rendered.

## Rule 4 — Versioned Identity

A downstream projection of a ROOT object must preserve at least:

- canonical source identifier;
- source contract/version;
- provenance;
- authority state;
- divergence status.

## Rule 5 — Divergence

A consumer must not silently overwrite a conflict.

When a ROOT identity/version and a dependent DOMAIN projection disagree, the dependent projection becomes unresolved/stale until explicitly reconciled.

Research label: `ROOT_DOMAIN_DIVERGENCE`.

This label is a governance contract candidate for machine enforcement; its exact runtime schema is not canonized by this document.

## Rule 6 — Promotion

Minimum research authority vocabulary:

`OBSERVED`, `DERIVED`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `CANDIDATE`, `VALIDATED`, `CANONICAL`, `UNRESOLVED`.

Promotion to CANONICAL requires explicit HNK authority. Derivation, numerical coincidence, symbolic resemblance and computational encoding are insufficient by themselves.

## Rule 7 — Legacy G01–G40

The existing 40-glyph inventory is preserved as historical/research material according to its existing provenance and authority.

Genesis is not required to return 40 fundamental units. No deletion/replacement occurs merely because a new architecture is under research.

## Rule 8 — SimpleWay Boundary

SimpleWay Math and SimpleWay-HNK are educational/experimental consumers. They may reveal evidence and generate candidates, but they do not promote HNK foundational or linguistic canon.

Evidence discovered there returns to the owning canon for evaluation.

## Rule 9 — Genesis Gate

No new fundamental HNK writing inventory is promoted before the Genesis gates for phonological census, comparative writing-system research, HNK-MATH/AK, Mandala census and architecture synthesis are satisfied.

## Rule 10 — Safety Against Accidental Canon

Research artifacts should default to non-canonical authority unless they explicitly cite the promotion decision that made them canonical.

Unknown fields and unsupported correspondences remain `UNRESOLVED`; absence is preferable to fabricated completeness.
