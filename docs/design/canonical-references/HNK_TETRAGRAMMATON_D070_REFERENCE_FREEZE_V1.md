## 4. Rights / provenance decision

The canonical SVG is authored inside HNK and therefore does not depend on licensing the received WebP.

The historical texts/formulae are provenance for the terminology/family, not a claim that they published this exact modern composition.

The received WebP remains evidence/fingerprint only and must not be redistributed by the canonical asset pipeline unless its rights are independently established later.

## 5. Epistemic / theological boundary

Within HNK, the Pantáculo is treated as a traditional/symbolic operator under God. Perceived protection, warmth, presence or boundary is phenomenology; it does not prove an impenetrable physical field and never replaces ordinary safety, medical care, legal care or prudent action.
