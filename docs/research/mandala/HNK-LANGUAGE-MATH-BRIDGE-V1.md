## Structural concentration

The complete geometric universe has:

- 256 coarse families
- 11,492 topological families
- 3,041 radial-angular families

The current language uses glyphs from only:

- 1 coarse family
- 4 topological families
- 5 radial-angular families

This means current HENUVOKODAN practice is structurally concentrated in the HNK40 Genesis neighborhood.

That observation does not imply that unused families are invalid. It means their linguistic role has not yet been established by the current corpus.

## Acquisition consequence

The acquisition architecture should distinguish:

### Core literacy

Teach the existing governed language surface first:

- high-frequency glyph identities;
- recovered lexemes;
- authored candidates according to their authority;
- grammar components according to their scoped governance;
- currently observed structural families.

### Expansion laboratory

Use structurally distant families as held-out experiments for:

- visual discrimination;
- productive rule learning;
- unseen-form inference;
- morphology hypotheses;
- execution-metadata experiments.

A new geometric family must not receive semantic or phonological meaning merely because it exists.
