# HNK-RESEARCH GENESIS V0.1 — Design Specification

Status: APPROVED RESEARCH DIRECTION
Authority: CODEX-HNK Root Canon governance
Date: 2026-09-26

## 1. Goal

Research and formalize the foundations for a mathematically structured, cabalistic, phonologically complete and multidimensional HNK writing system without preserving any historical glyph count as a design constraint.

The existing G01–G40 inventory is research heritage, not a required target count.

## 2. Authority Boundary

### CODEX-HNK — ROOT CANON
Owns HNK ontological and mathematical foundations:
- HNK-MATH;
- Atomic-Kode (AK) model;
- Mandala HNK geometry;
- mathematical coordinates and transformations;
- canonical symbolic correspondences;
- provenance and authority states;
- canonical contracts exported to domain consumers.

### HNK-KODE — DOMAIN CANON
Owns the operational linguistic system, subordinate to CODEX-HNK foundational contracts:
- phonology and phonotactics;
- writing-system composition;
- transliteration;
- linguistic glyph manifestations;
- lexicon and grammar;
- linguistic encoding/decoding surfaces.

HNK-KODE MUST NOT independently redefine an AK, Mandala geometry, mathematical identity or canonical HNK correspondence owned by CODEX-HNK.

### SimpleWay Math — EDUCATIONAL CONSUMER / LAB
May teach, visualize, experiment with and test mathematical structures. Discoveries return to CODEX-HNK as research/candidates. SimpleWay Math does not canonize HNK-MATH.

## 3. Governance States

Every researched assertion/correspondence MUST preserve provenance and authority. Minimum states:

- OBSERVED — directly present in a cited source/artifact.
- DERIVED — mechanically/mathematically derived from declared premises.
- HYPOTHESIS — proposed relationship requiring investigation.
- CANDIDATE — authored proposal admitted for formal evaluation.
- VALIDATED — passed its declared validation gate.
- CANONICAL — explicitly promoted by HNK authority.
- UNRESOLVED — evidence is insufficient or conflicting.

A numerical coincidence, visual similarity or attractive symbolic analogy MUST NOT promote itself to CANONICAL.

## 4. Fundamental Design Premise

Mandala HNK is modeled as:

**finite fundamental core + extensible compositional space**.

The research MUST NOT assume that one historical glyph equals one Mandala cell. It MUST test whether linguistic units correspond to individual AKs, groups, paths, transformations, coordinates or another reproducible structure.

## 5. Primary Architecture Candidate

Primary candidate: **Compositional Multidimensional Writing System**.

Alphabetic-atomic and syllabic/moraic systems remain comparison controls and MUST NOT be discarded before evaluation.

Provisional research layers (names are not automatically canonical terms):

- L0 AK — minimal mathematical/addressable unit.
- L1 PHON — phonetic properties.
- L2 KODE — valid graphical/phonological composition.
- L3 BLOCK — pronounceable syllabic/moraic composition.
- L4 LEXEME — linguistic word/lexical structure.
- L5 SIGIL — symbolic/semantic projection.
- L6 MANDALA — global relational/transformation space.

## 6. Representation Surfaces

A canonical object may expose multiple surfaces without conflating encoding with symbolic meaning:

- linear text;
- glyph/2D geometry;
- phonetic/IPA representation;
- sound/audio parameters;
- color;
- numeric ID;
- binary;
- hexadecimal;
- matrix / QR-like encoding;
- polar coordinates;
- Cartesian coordinates;
- isometric/3D representation;
- Mandala path/position.

Surfaces declared as encodings SHOULD be deterministic and reversible where technically possible.

Example research invariant:

`KODE <-> numeric identity <-> BIN <-> HEX <-> AK coordinate`

Symbolic correspondences are separately typed and need not be bijective.

## 7. Comparative Research Corpus

The Genesis research MUST compare at least:

### Linguistic / writing systems
- Hebrew;
- Greek;
- Latin alphabet and IPA;
- Hiragana and Katakana (moraic/syllabic organization);
- Hangul (feature-aware geometric block composition);
- Arabic;
- representative abugida structures;
- relevant constructed writing systems.

### Mathematical / symbolic
- Cabalistic structures and the HNK Mandala source material;
- Tree/Sefirot structures where source-supported;
- I Ching binary construction: two line states, 8 trigrams, 64 hexagrams;
- combinatorics;
- modular structures;
- symmetry and transformations.

### Computational
- binary representation;
- hexadecimal representation;
- Unicode/character encoding constraints;
- two-dimensional matrix encoding;
- QR Code principles including positioning, redundancy and error correction as comparative engineering concepts.

### Spatial
- polar geometry;
- Cartesian geometry;
- isometric projection;
- 3D coordinates;
- rotation/reflection/transformation.

No historical or esoteric correspondence is accepted merely because it shares a number with another system.

## 8. Phonological Census Gate

No new fundamental glyph inventory may be canonized before a phonological census of the validated HNK corpus.

The census MUST identify:
- vowels;
- consonants;
- attested phonemes/allophones where evidence permits;
- syllable/mora patterns;
- consonant clusters;
- phonotactic restrictions supported by evidence;
- frequency/coverage across the canonical corpus;
- unresolved pronunciations;
- distinction between HNK-core requirements and optional external transcription requirements.

Research target:

**the smallest compositional inventory that can encode 100% of the currently validated HNK corpus without loss, while allowing a separately governed extension mechanism for external phonetic transcription.**

## 9. Mandala / Atomic-Kode Census Gate

Before mapping new linguistic glyphs onto the Mandala, research MUST census the reference artifacts:
- rings;
- angular sectors;
- boundaries;
- repeated structures;
- minimal addressable cells;
- symbols/colors/text visibly present;
- source provenance;
- geometric relations.

A provisional coordinate may be investigated as:

`AK = (ring, sector, layer, state)`

with derived spatial coordinates such as `(r, theta, z)`, but this tuple is a research model until validated.

## 10. Genesis Subprojects

V0.1 is intentionally decomposed into independently reviewable workstreams:

1. Governance Boundary.
2. Canonical HNK Phonological Census.
3. Comparative Writing Systems Matrix.
4. HNK-MATH / Atomic-Kode formal model.
5. Mandala Census and geometric reconstruction.
6. Encoding Surfaces: numeric/BIN/HEX/matrix/QR-like.
7. Spatial Surface: isometric/3D projection and reversible coordinates.
8. Genesis Synthesis: compare alphabetic, syllabic and compositional architectures.
9. Only after synthesis: propose a new fundamental glyph/component inventory.

## 11. Non-Goals for V0.1

V0.1 MUST NOT:
- force the system to contain 40 fundamental glyphs;
- replace G01–G40 without evidence;
- invent missing HNK pronunciations;
- assign arbitrary spiritual meanings to binary/HEX values;
- declare I Ching-to-HNK equivalences from numerology alone;
- copy QR Code visually and call it HNK;
- treat a reference Mandala image as a mathematically verified schema before census;
- let SimpleWay Math become the authority for HNK-MATH;
- create new linguistic canon merely to fill a mathematical grid.

## 12. Success Criteria

Genesis V0.1 succeeds when it can produce, with provenance:

1. an auditable phonological inventory of known HNK;
2. a machine-readable AK/Mandala research model;
3. a comparative matrix explaining which external systems solve which representation problems;
4. deterministic encoding contracts for surfaces classified as encodings;
5. explicit unresolved regions rather than fabricated correspondences;
6. an evidence-backed recommendation for the architecture and size/composition of the next HNK writing-system inventory.

The final number of fundamental units MUST emerge from the evidence and architecture rather than precede them.
