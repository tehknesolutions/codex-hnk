## 1. Purpose

Create the first controlled lexical candidate batch for the six operational Chromatic Genesis energies and four meta-principles.

Nothing in this document is promoted to HNK_CANON. The forms below are authored research candidates generated under the observed `@hnk/linguas` safe surface inventory and governance constraints.

## 2. Linguistic constraints used

Observed safe surface units from `phonological-census-v0.1.mjs`:

`A E I O U H M N L R B D P T K S TS V Z Y`

The census itself is `RESEARCH_NON_CANONICAL`; therefore this batch treats the inventory as a safe authoring constraint, not an eternal phonological law.

Additional governance inherited from `grammar-core-v1.mjs` and `authored.mjs`:

- semantics before form selection;
- no automatic productivity;
- no numerology-based form selection;
- no silent historical-recovery claim;
- authored primitives may exist without pretending to be recovered morphemes;
- every broader morphological rule requires separate evidence/governance;
- candidate status is not canonical promotion.

## 3. Candidate-generation policy

Each concept receives three candidate forms.

All candidates in v0.1 are intentionally treated as `AUTHORED_PRIMITIVE_ENERGY` rather than decomposed pseudo-etymologies. This avoids inventing productive morphemes that HNK-KODE has not licensed.

The candidate forms aim to:

1. use only safe surface units;
2. remain pronounceable in the observed HNK corpus style;
3. avoid direct English/Portuguese color-name transliteration;
4. encode the semantic principle, not the gemstone name;
5. remain distinct from known high-salience corpus forms inspected during Matrix 004;
6. remain subject to collision checks against the full master lexicon before Human Gate.

IPA below is a provisional transparent reading guide, NOT a canonical phonological binding. Final IPA must be generated/validated through `@hnk/glyphs` G-ID phoneme bindings.
