## Purpose

Create a provenance-preserving namespace for Ministério Amor e Verdade (MAV) theological, ecclesiological and historical research. Material stored here is not automatically HNK canon and is not automatically asserted as external historical fact.

4. Teacher as biblical tutor and reproducer of teachers.
5. Antioch as a church-formation/sending case study.
6. Spiritual authority versus juridical/institutional authority.
7. Church versus building/temple.
8. NT economic support and tithe audit.
9. Participatory liturgy and community order.
10. Unity, faction and denominational identity.
11. Constantine and Christian institutionalization.
12. Christian images, devotional development and icon veneration.

## Governance

Each claim should distinguish:
- biblical text/source;
- historical source;
- MAV/TW legacy source;
- TW testimony where applicable;
- TW interpretation;
- HNK/MAV synthesis;
- unresolved or competing interpretation.

Historical and scientific claims remain research until appropriately sourced and reviewed. HNK-internal definitions follow the applicable creator/Human Gate authority.
