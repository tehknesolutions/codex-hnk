# MAV Ecclesiology and Ministry Research Matrix V1

Status: RESEARCH MATRIX — NOT AUTOMATIC CANON

| Domain | Current MAV/TW working synthesis | Evidence/review need |
|---|---|---|
| Berean method | Human teaching and prophetic material remain examinable; self-critique uses the same standard applied to others. | Biblical exegesis + legacy MAV source audit |
| Church/Body | Ministry arises to equip needs of the Body rather than making the Body exist to maintain ministers. | NT corpus review |
| Five ministries | Evangelist reaches; prophet testifies/exhorts/discerns; apostle establishes/forms/sends; teacher teaches/reproduces teachers; pastor disciples/cares/intercedes. | Eph 4 + Acts + pastoral/Pauline corpus |
| Ministry recognition | Function/service/fruit precede title; recognition follows observable service and fruit. | NT leadership vocabulary audit |
| Teacher | Biblical tutor who equips people to study Scripture and teach others/new converts. | 2 Tim 2:2 and teaching corpus |
| Antioch | Receive ministry → mature → develop ministers → send ministry; maturity does not mean isolation. | Acts 11/13/15 case study |
| Authority | Spiritual authority is distinguished from merely juridical/institutional office; Christian authority is service-oriented rather than domination-oriented. | Gospel + epistle review; MAV/Dyer provenance |
| Building/temple | Church is the people/Body; meeting infrastructure is not identical to the Church and is not automatically sinful. | 1 Chr 17, Acts 7, NT ekklesia/temple study |
| Economics | Audit compulsory Christian tithe and map NT support toward poor, widows, needy, mission, ministers, churches and infrastructure. | Full OT/NT economic study |
| Liturgy | Community order should not erase participation of the Body; freedom and order must both be examined. | 1 Cor 11–14 + early-Christian history |
| Unity/factions | Pauline anti-faction texts are relevant to denominational identity, but modern application is an interpretive extension. | 1 Cor 1/3 + historical/ecclesiological analysis |
| Constantine | Constantinian patronage/legal-political transformation is a historical research axis; episcopacy predates Constantine and unified-conspiracy claims require evidence. | Primary/modern historical sources |
| Images/icons | Christian art predates Constantine; image, devotional image and veneration must be historically distinguished. | Archaeology + patristic/iconoclast/Nicaea II sources |

## Non-destructive rule

Legacy MAV books, site pages, studies and teachings should be imported as primary TW/MAV sources with date/version/provenance. Later research may confirm, refine, supersede or reject a claim without erasing the historical source.

## Promotion rule

RESEARCH → SOURCED CLAIM → CONTRADICTORY REVIEW → MAV SYNTHESIS → optional HNK CANDIDATE → explicit Human Gate → HNK CANON.

No row in this matrix is promoted merely by appearing here.
